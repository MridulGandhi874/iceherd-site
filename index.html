Ice Herd — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

What Ice Herd stores

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, skins, streaks and settings) is saved on your device. You can play without an account.

If you sign in

Signing in with Google is optional. If you do, we use Google Firebase to store: your Google account ID and email address (used only to sign you in), your game save (so it syncs across your devices), the player name and avatar you choose, and your scores for leaderboards, the daily puzzle, tournaments and friend races. Your player name, avatar and scores are visible to other players. Your email and save are never shown to anyone.

Advertising

Ice Herd shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and information about how ads are viewed and tapped. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice any time under Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Children

Ice Herd is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting your data

Without an account, uninstalling Ice Herd (or clearing its storage) deletes everything. With an account, open the app and use Settings → Delete account to permanently delete your account, cloud save, player name and scores. You can also email mridulgandhi874@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, and we will delete it within 30 days.
